JavaScript Operators

JavaScript Operators are special symbols or keywords used to perform operations on values and variables. They are used to calculate, compare, assign, and manipulate data.

Common Types of JavaScript Operators :

  1. Arithmetic Operators (+, -, *, /, %, **)

  2. Assignment Operators (=, +=, -=, *=, /=)

  3. Comparison Operators (==, ===, !=, >, <, >=, <=)

  4. Logical Operators (&&, ||, !)

  5. Increment/Decrement Operators (++, --)

Arithmetic Operators :

Arithmetic Operators are used to perform mathematical calculations on numbers, such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and finding the remainder.

+ Addition 10 + 5 → 15
- Subtraction 10 - 5 → 5
* Multiplication 10 * 5 → 50
/ Division 10 / 5 → 2
% Modulus (Remainder) 10 % 3 → 1
** Exponentiation (Power) 2 ** 3 → 8
++ Increment a++
-- Decrement a--

Assignment Operators :

Assignment Operators are used to assign values to variables. They can also combine assignment with arithmetic operations.

= Assign value a = 10
+= Add and assign a += 5
-= Subtract and assign a -= 5
*= Multiply and assign a *= 5
/= Divide and assign a /= 5
%= Modulus and assign a %= 5
**= Exponentiation and assign a **= 2

Comparission Operators :

Comparison Operators are used to compare two values and return a Boolean result (true or false).

== Equal to 10 == "10" → true
=== Strictly equal to 10 === "10" → false
!= Not equal to 10 != 5 → true
!== Strictly not equal to 10 !== "10" → true
> Greater than 10 > 5 → true
< Less than 10 < 5 → false
>= Greater than or equal to 10 >= 10 → true
<= Less than or equal to 10 <= 5 → false

Logical Operators :

Logical Operators are used to combine or evaluate multiple conditions and return a Boolean value (true or false).

&& Logical AND (10 > 5 && 8 > 3) → true
|| Logical OR (10 < 5 || 8 > 3) → true
! Logical NOT !(10 > 5) → false
